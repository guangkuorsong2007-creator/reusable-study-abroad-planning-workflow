## The first useful completion point / 首个有用的完成点

A user can explain their present position, compare a manageable set of plausible route families, understand what remains uncertain, choose a provisional direction or a specific next investigation, and resume later using a saved state. They need not choose one country immediately. Exploration that identifies the decisive missing evidence can be a useful result.

The full education–work–residence chain must be visible, with each transition evaluated separately. A plausible education route does not establish work access or a residence outcome. Later results are updated when actual evidence arrives. This first cycle does not purport to complete years of preparation or deliver admission/employment/status.

## Proposed user journey / 试验流程

1. **Start and check capabilities.** Establish language, present decision and scope. Verify whether this environment can read intended inputs, retrieve official evidence, calculate and preserve state. Keep unavailable abilities explicit. Use a harmless synthetic state to check persistence/resumption before relying on it. No new subscriptions, installations or integrations assumed.
2. **A short initial interview.** Cover stage/qualification, timing, all-inclusive funding facts, main motivations, non-negotiable constraints and current uncertainties. Ask only questions that affect the next comparison. Explain unfamiliar terms; “not sure yet” remains valid. Read supplied material before asking the person to repeat it.
3. **Establish minimum evidence.** Extract relevant achieved results and curriculum/experience. Distinguish totals, subsets, attempts, grade scales and planned results. Identify major conflicts. Do useful exploration while explicitly reserving any eligibility conclusion dependent on missing evidence.
4. **Compare routes at two levels.** First compare country/region and study/work route families using the user's real priorities. Then deepen plausible institution/program/occupation options. Do not research every combination or use invented points/probabilities. Cover cost scope, academic fit, work-related conditions, residence-related dependencies and quality-of-life tradeoffs with relevant evidence.
5. **Make a provisional choice or evidence plan.** Explain reasons, alternatives and what finding would change the preferred direction. The user may decide to investigate one blocking fact rather than select a destination. An agent recommendation is recorded as a proposal until the user accepts it.
6. **Save a useful checkpoint.** Give current facts/decisions, evidence IDs, unresolved conditions, next actions and where the detail is. Each action has an output and dependency, not an invented daily schedule. Preserve relevant preparation across still-plausible routes.
7. **Resume and update.** A fresh context identifies the present task and known facts without repeating the full interview. New grades, funding facts or rules mark affected conclusions for review. Unrelated conclusions remain intact.

For file-capable tools, the private canonical record can be local. For chat-only tools, the user may save and carry a sanitized checkpoint. Moving private material between tools is a deliberate user data-sharing choice; no automatic cross-tool transfer is assumed. Actual persistence and source access must be reported by observed results.

## Case-record skeleton / 个案记录骨架

- **Current decision:** question; desired result; language and explanation needs.
- **Priorities/constraints:** relative priorities, hard constraints, flexible preferences, undecided fields, who confirmed them and when.
- **Facts:** value; original meaning/units/course or time set; actual versus planned; evidence label and source location.
- **Evidence:** claim; exact source/page; jurisdiction/institution/program and applicant category; publication/access/effective dates; support and limits; blocked/unread states.
- **Alternatives:** why worth considering; known conditions; unknowns; total-cost scope; work/residence dependencies; fit tradeoffs; next evidence.
- **Decision history:** user's accepted choice and rationale; conditions for revisiting; clearly superseded prior choices.
- **Actions:** output; dependency; relevant verified date if any; current completion status.
- **Checkpoint:** current task; what changed; unresolved questions; next useful step; evidence limitations; pointers to details.

Keep the authoritative fact in one place and reference it elsewhere. Never silently replace an actual result with a target, or erase a conflict to produce a cleaner comparison.

## Portable prompt drafts / 通用提示词草稿

The Chinese prompts below are intended for the proposed Chinese-undergraduate pilot. They are task instructions, not claims that every tool supports them.

**Start / 开始**

> 请帮助我进行留学—就业—长期居留规划。先明确我本轮要解决的决策，读取我已授权提供的个案记录，说明你实际能读取、检索、计算和保存哪些内容。按当前决策分批提问；我不知道的偏好可以先通过比较来探索。不要默认国家、专业、成绩换算或未来政策。重要事实注明来源、适用对象、日期和局限；建议、假设、用户决定与已完成结果分开记录。结束时给我可保存的进度和下一步。

**Resume / 接续**

> 请实际读取以下最新进度及其指向的相关材料；读取不到请说明。从当前任务继续，先识别已确认事实、已作决定、未决问题和下一步。只问当前材料未回答且会影响本轮决策的问题。旧报告不自动等于当前规则，目标成绩不等于实际成绩。结束时保存或提供更新后的进度，并说明实际保存位置或需要我手动保存的内容。

**Review / 复核**

> 请逐项复核关键结论：输入是否准确完整，计算是否可复现，来源是否真正支持该结论，规则是否适用于此人和时间，建议是否符合其目标。分别标记支持、条件成立、未解决、与证据冲突。不能把算术正确扩展成录取、就业或居留可行性已确认。不要替用户更改已确认偏好。输出需要纠正的具体结论、原因、影响和最小修复方案。

## Trial cases / 试验案例

All cases below are synthetic test specifications, not country rules or actual applicant records. Start with these; an actual participant would later need a separately agreed process and data scope. No real tester is recruited or contacted by this document.

- **SYN-UG-001: open destination.** Chinese undergraduate; budget and desired degree timing stated, career interests uncertain, language diagnostic absent. Check that the interview can proceed without forcing a country or inventing language/subject competence.
- **SYN-UG-002: conflicting metrics.** Records contain a semester percentage, a differently scoped GPA and a planned retake. Check that distinct metrics remain distinct, and foreign conversion/disclosure are left unresolved without an applicable rule.
- **SYN-UG-003: tool change.** Give a fresh tool only the checkpoint and accessible referenced materials. Check correct continuation, honest limits and no unnecessary repeated intake. A manual user-assisted transfer is an explicit mode, not automatic synchronization.
- **SYN-UG-004: changed input.** Replace a funding fact or introduce a clearly marked fictional rule update with an effective date. Check that affected claims are flagged and future rules do not become current rules. Fictional source material must never be presented as real official evidence.

## Acceptance criteria / 验收标准

A trial is ready for review when:

- The current decision, confirmed facts and accepted priorities match the case; unknowns remain visible.
- Important factual claims have applicable evidence or are explicitly unverified. A URL alone does not mark a claim verified; blocked pages remain unread.
- Calculations disclose their inputs/definitions, distinguish actual and target results, and reproduce under the stated assumptions.
- Every option's education/work/residence conditions are separated; no later outcome is inferred from an earlier-stage minimum.
- The user receives understandable tradeoffs and a useful next action or evidence request. A final country choice is optional.
- A fresh context can recover the current task, accepted decisions and next step; missing tool capabilities are disclosed.
- An input change prompts review of the affected conclusions, preserving history and unrelated work.
- No actual case data is mixed into shared/public materials and no external action is performed without the relevant authorization.

Record failures, repairs, unnecessary repeated questions, untraceable claims, user explanation difficulties and actual research effort. Do not claim measured savings or user satisfaction without observations. An unperformed country comparison is not counted as successful multi-country validation.
